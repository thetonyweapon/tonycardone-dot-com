# America First Field

## Stadium
A utilitarian stadium with the best backdrop in the league. From the upper decks of the stadium, you get blasted with sunlight and a gorgeous sunset over the mountains.

Beverage selection is limited mostly due to Utah laws it seems, but the RSL "fight song" is a good one right before kickoff.

Stadium access is available via the Sandy Expo station on the TRAX Blue Line. This is walkable (10-15 minutes) but if you have accessiblity concerns, I would recommend a rideshare instead.

Unfortunately, the stadium site is surrounded by a whole lot of nothingness, so be sure to pregame and postgame in downtown Salt Lake City

## City
Salt Lake City is very underrated as a city. It's got all the outdoorsy options as Denver, but at a much better price point. It does have its eccentricities being in Utah, namely being all the good beer has to be served in cans, but you will probably be able to find all the typical locations you need. Just be prepared to walk a bit more than you'd expect since their city blocks are much longer than the normal block.

It does get a bit funky on Sundays due to many Mormon-owned establishments being closed and the influx of parishoners attending church services in the downtown area, but the city itself is very progressive. One year, you could walk from the Tabernacle Choir performance on Sunday morning directly to the Pride Parade a few blocks away.

The State Capitol is a good free trip, and a lovely vantage point over the region atop the hill. I always found it poetic that the State Capitol and the Mormon Temple are blocks away from one another, with the government buildings keeping watch over the religious complex.

The Natural History Museum of Utah is an enlightening trip out at the University of Utah, and you can stop by Rice-Eccles Stadium if you're in the area to see the once and future Olympic Stadium.

A great beer spot is the [Beerhive Pub](https://beerhivepub.edan.io/) in the downwtown business district.

Outside the city, you can make a week trip out of the national parks and state parks nearby. Arches National Park, Canyonlands, and Park City are highlights, with a special mention for me for Bear Lake and Cache Valley for some lesser-known areas. There's also the super popular Zion and Bryce Canyon that I haven't visited but are among the most popular.

## Transit
The [Utah Transit Authority](https://www.rideuta.com/) runs light rail service every 15 minutes from SLC and towards the stadium. Bus coverage outside the light rail is spotty, so plan accordingly.

No tap-to-pay, so you will need to pay through the Transit app or by getting a ticket at a light rail station.

## Running
I haven't yet done enough running/walking to provide a review, but Ensign Peak is a close trailhead to downtown with a short walk. The city is filled with great trails, but I haven't been able to use them yet.

## Overall
Don't sleep on Salt Lake City. It may not have the headlines of some other cities, but there is much to do, and so many outdoor options that can turn a soccer trip into a national park trip with the right amount of preparation.
