# Allianz Field

## Stadium
Allianz Field is located in the Midway neighborhood of the Twin Cities, and is a great stadium by all accounts. The sightlines are perfect from every spot, but I have to warn about some of the availability of beer selections in the big bar area. It's gotten better in recent years, however, so big points there. It does suffer from what many MLS stadiums do these days, in that the concourses are too crowded at peak times and the facilities are overwhelmed.

The stadium site itself is nothing to write about, with a cool loon statue being in the corner of the plot, but the rest is still under development. The site is adjacent to probably the best bar in the world, the [Black Hart](https://www.blackhartstp.com/), which welcomes away fans before and after the game. Protip: know how you're getting home, because the locals will ensure you have such a good time, you'll lose track of time.

## City
Minneapolis-Saint Paul is such an interesting metro area. On the eastern edge is Saint Paul, the seat of government, and a town that has limited downtown options. In my experience, it's not a very lively downtown, but there are some excellent breweries in the downtown core. For sports fans, CHS Field, which hosts the minor league Saint Paul Saints, and the Grand Casino Arena, which hosts the zero-time NHL champion Minnesota Wild and two-time PWHL champion Minnesota Frost.

Minneapolis is a more traditional big city feel, with nightlife being open later with more variety. As someone who prefers a more social venue, I'd recommend [Gluek's](https://www.glueks.com/). The Nicollet Mall centers most of the entertainment, though be sure to venture out to Bde Maka Ska and Lake of the Isles, a beautiful recreation area not too far from downtown, and Minnehaha Falls.

## Transit
[Metro Transit](https://www.metrotransit.org/) runs frequent, affordable service basically everywhere of note in the Twin Cities. It's one of the best in the business, with a direct line to MSP and to the stadium. Be warned, however, that the Green Line takes much longer than you'd expect, since it doesn't always have signal priority. You can use the Metro Transit app, or purchase fares at each light rail station, but can't use tap-to-pay. So if you're riding the bus and don't pick up a fare at a light rail station, you will need to use the app.

## Running
It puts all other cities to shame. Particularly in Minneapolis, there are trails along the Mississippi River of any distance you choose. There are no conflict crossings with vehicles, and even have separate bike and pedestrian pathways in key sections with high traffic. There's no better running experience than a crisp spring/fall morning in the Minnesota sun that lets you pick up a coffee on the walk/train back to the hotel.

## Overall
This is always a trip to bookmark when the schedule comes out. In fact, it's one of a few cities that I have explicitly spent extra time in while working remotely to experience the full region's offerings. Particularly when the weather is nice, I don't think any other trip compares.
