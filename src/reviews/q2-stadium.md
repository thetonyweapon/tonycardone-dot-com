# Q2 Stadium

## Stadium
One of the best soccer-specific stadiums in the country. The design is intentionally intimate with steep stands that amplify crowd noise. The supporters section behind the goal is electric. Premium sight lines from every seat and excellent craft beer selection throughout the concourse.

## City
Austin's vibrant east side is a short walk or ride from the stadium, with some of the best food trucks and music venues in the city. The stadium has helped revitalize the surrounding neighborhood, and match days feel like a true community event.

## Transit
Multiple bus routes serve the area and ride share is reliable but can surge after matches. Limited parking in the immediate vicinity means most people walk from nearby lots or take a rideshare. The city could do more with dedicated shuttle service.

## Running
The Ann and Roy Butler Hike and Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake is about a 15-minute jog from the stadium. There are also good running routes through the nearby Mueller neighborhood. The stadium itself is in a walkable area that's fun to explore on foot before a match.

## Overall
An exceptional gameday experience from start to finish. The stadium is world-class, the city embraces match day, and the surrounding area is genuinely fun to explore. A destination for any soccer fan.
